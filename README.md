## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end Data Performance Analysis on a seasonal agricultural dataset comprising **4,000 farm records and 28 attributes**. The study focuses on evaluating environmental variables, soil health, irrigation efficiency, and crop inputs to determine key factors influencing yield and overall farm profitability across different seasons (Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid).

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives & Analytical Coverage
- **Data Preprocessing & Cleaning:** Handled missing values, verified column data types, inspected duplicates, and treated extreme outliers.
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):**
  - **Univariate Analysis:** Seasonal distribution (Pie Chart/Bar Plot), Yield & Profit distributions (Histograms with KDE).
  - **Bivariate Analysis:** Rainfall vs. Yield, Farm Area vs. Production (Scatter Plots with Regression Lines).
  - **Multivariate Analysis:** Comprehensive Pairplots categorizing environmental and performance metrics across seasons.
- **Strategic Recommendations:** Formulated data-backed insights to optimize crop selection, improve resource usage, and mitigate seasonal risks.

## 📊 Summary of Insights
1. **Seasonal Variation:** Crop yields and overall profit show strong dependency on seasonal rainfall and climate conditions.
2. **Irrigation Impact:** Modern irrigation methods (Drip/Sprinkler) demonstrate higher resource efficiency and yield compared to traditional flood irrigation.
3. **Risk Mitigation:** Managing disease and pest risks promptly is crucial for sustaining high profit margins across farm sizes.
